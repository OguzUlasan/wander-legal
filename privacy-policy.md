# Privacy Policy — Wander

**Last updated: 12 September 2026**

## The short version

- You don’t need an account to use Wander. Signing in is optional and only for walking with friends.
- Your focus sessions and progress live on your device. We also keep a copy under a random anonymous id, so a reinstall doesn’t lose your trail.
- If you use groups or friends, other travellers see the nickname you chose, your traveller, and how your walk is going — never your name or email.
- We use a small number of services to run the app (Google Firebase, RevenueCat, Apple, Expo, Sentry). We do not sell your data and we do not track you across other apps.
- You can delete your account and everything we hold from inside the app.

## Information you provide

A nickname for your traveller, and settings such as your daily goal, timer defaults, chosen hour, season and sounds. Stored on your device; the nickname is also what other travellers see in a group or as your friend.

Optional task labels you type, and your session lengths and outcomes. Stored on your device and copied to our servers under your anonymous id (see “What we keep on our servers”).

If you email us, we receive your message and address so we can reply.

## What we keep on our servers

Wander creates a random anonymous account for you on Google Firebase the first time it runs. Under that id we store a copy of your finished sessions (start, end, focused minutes, mode, task label, trail), your Steps ledger, your streak freezes, your unlocks, your push-notification token if you allow notifications, and your block list. This copy is not optional — it is what restores your progress after a reinstall or when you sign in on a new phone, and it lets us compute group bonuses honestly.

A server-side recomputation of your Steps and streak may also be stored, so that what other travellers see in a group cannot be inflated by editing a device.

## Signing in (optional)

Groups and friends need a stable identity, so those features ask you to sign in with Apple or Google. Apple and Google send us a token and, depending on your choice, your name and email; Apple lets you hide your email. Firebase Authentication stores these on your account. We use them only to link your existing progress to your sign-in — they are never shown to other travellers, never used for marketing, and never sold.

You can keep using every timer, streak, badge and stat feature without ever signing in.

## Walking with others

- Group journeys: while a group is open, its members — and anyone who has the group’s 6-letter invite code — can see your nickname, your traveller, whether you are focusing, paused or finished, and your focused minutes for that walk. Group records are deleted within a week of the walk ending.
- Friends: when you pair with a friend by code, you both see each other’s nickname, traveller, shared streak and the small preset reactions (a wave, a campfire, a trail sign) you send each other. There is no free-text chat in Wander.
- Travellers walking now: while you focus, an anonymous “focusing” flag under your id feeds a public count of how many travellers are walking right now. The count is a number; no one can see who is in it.
- Invite and friend codes are short random codes that point to your group or your account; they contain no personal information.
- Safety: you can block another traveller (they stop appearing for you) and report a group member. A report — your id, their id, the group id and the reason — is emailed to us and kept as a moderation record.

## Collected automatically

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone audio or health data. Seasons and the time of day in the scene come from your device’s clock and calendar, not from where you are.

- Usage analytics via Google Firebase Analytics: app events (such as session started or badge earned), device model, iOS version, app version, language, an app-instance id, and your anonymous account id so events from one account line up. No advertising identifier is read and no tracking permission is requested; there are no ads in Wander.
- Crash and error reports via Sentry: the technical trace of what went wrong, device model, iOS version and app version. No names, nicknames or task labels are attached.
- Purchase status via RevenueCat and Apple: whether you hold Wander Pro and the related transaction ids, so Pro can be restored on any of your devices.
- Push notifications: if you allow notifications and use friends, a device token is stored on our servers and messages are delivered through Expo’s push service.

## Who processes data for us

Google Firebase (authentication, database, realtime presence, analytics, cloud functions and an email extension that forwards reports to us), RevenueCat (subscription status), Apple (in-app purchases and the App Store), Expo (push notification delivery) and Sentry (crash reports). Each processes data on our behalf under its own terms and may do so outside your country. We do not sell your data or share it for third-party advertising.

## Retention

On-device data stays until you delete it or remove the app. Server copies stay while your account exists. Group records are removed within a week of a walk ending, and lobbies that never start are removed after a day. Analytics and crash data are kept for the retention period of the provider and then deleted or aggregated. Moderation records are kept as long as needed to keep the community safe.

## Your choices

Notifications are opt-in and can be turned off any time in Profile or in iOS Settings. Analytics and crash reporting cannot be switched off separately from the app; deleting your account removes the link between your id and past events.

You can stop using groups and friends at any time; blocking a traveller is immediate and one-sided.

## Deleting your data

Profile → Data → Delete account & data erases everything on your device and asks our servers to remove your account and all the data listed above: sessions, Steps, unlocks, push token, block list, friend pairs, your invite codes, your group memberships and your presence. If your phone is offline the server step may not complete; the app will tell you, and you can email us to finish it.

What remains afterwards: moderation records (ids and a reason, no names), and purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies. Deleting the app also removes all locally stored data.

## Children

Wander is not directed to children under 13 (or your country’s minimum age of digital consent), and we don’t knowingly collect their data. Contact us if you believe a child has provided data and we will delete it.

Data may be processed in Türkiye and wherever our providers operate, which may have different data-protection laws. We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date reflects changes, and material changes will be noted in the app.
