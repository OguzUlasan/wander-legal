## Common questions

**My timer kept running while my screen was locked. Is that right?**
Yes. Wander measures focus by the clock, not by a running counter, so locking your phone or switching apps never loses a minute. The Lock Screen shows your walk as a Live Activity.

**I bought Wander Pro but it isn’t showing.**
Open Profile → About → *Restore purchases*. Pro is tied to your Apple ID, so it restores on any of your devices. If restore says nothing was found, check you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
In iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Wander. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

**How do I delete my account and data?**
In the app: Profile → Data → *Delete account & data*. This erases everything on your device and asks our servers to remove your account and all data we hold. If your phone was offline and the server step could not complete, email us and we will finish it. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/) for exactly what is deleted.

**Someone in a group used an offensive nickname.**
Long-press their traveller in the group to report or block them. Reports reach us by email and we act on them, typically within 24 hours.

**Does Wander sell Steps or any other currency?**
No. Steps are only ever earned by focusing.
